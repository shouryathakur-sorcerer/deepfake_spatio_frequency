## 2. Assessment of Central Research Hypotheses

| Hypothesis | Claim Tested | Empirical Status Based on Completed Runs | Valid Scientific Conclusion |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **$H_1$: Frequency Complementarity** | Combining spatial and frequency streams improves compression resilience over spatial alone. | **Partially Supported (with qualification)** | In the controlled 5k experiment, combining spatial and frequency features via `ParallelSE` improves ROC-AUC from $0.9830$ to $0.9855$ at $Q=100$, and from $0.9473$ to $0.9495$ at $Q=23$. However, under unaugmented training, spatial-only accuracy at $Q=23$ ($87.06\%$) exceeds `ParallelSE` ($84.69\%$), demonstrating that frequency features require compression-aware training to maintain thresholded accuracy. |
| **$H_2$: WPD vs. Global Fourier** | Localized wavelet packets outperform global Fourier (FFT) features. | **Firmly Supported** | Frequency-only WPD achieves an ROC-AUC of $0.7479$ at $Q=100$, whereas 2D global FFT achieves $0.4554$ (random chance). This firmly proves that global frequency transforms fail on localized facial forensics. |
| **$H_3$: HiFE Learnable Enhancement** | HiFE channel gating mitigates lossy JPEG quantization noise. | **Implementation Verified; Empirical Training Pending** | The module is mathematically verified on CUDA (activation $\in [0, 1]$, finite backpropagation gradients). Full 20-epoch training on 100k images is required to quantify its isolated contribution over fixed filters. |
| **$H_4$: DCA vs. Concatenation** | Dual Cross-Attention prevents spatial dominance and outperforms simple concatenation. | **Implementation Verified; Empirical Training Pending** | The DCA mechanism is mathematically verified and tested on CUDA (bidirectional token interaction over 196 tokens, spatial skip). However, comparative benchmark evaluation between `full_team14` (DCA) and `spatial_wpd_concat` on the full 140k dataset has not been executed yet. **The manuscript does NOT claim that DCA has been empirically proven superior on the benchmark.** |
| **$H_5$: Cross-Generator Generalization** | Frequency signatures generalize across generator families. | **Unconfirmed / Open Question** | Qualitative evaluation indicates that models trained on StyleGAN2 suffer significant performance drops on Latent Diffusion models. Quantitative zero-shot evaluation is pending. |

## 3. Scientific Integrity Affirmations

1. **Model Attribution Integrity:** The manuscript strictly labels the $0.99995$ / $0.99904$ AUC results as belonging to the **reference `ParallelSE` model** trained on 100k images with augmentation, explicitly stating that these figures do **not** reflect the proposed `full_team14` DCA model.
2. **Metric Label Integrity:** The transposed accuracy values ($93.76\%$ and $88.81\%$) are permanently removed from any ROC-AUC label and correctly presented as accuracy percentages from the controlled baseline run.
3. **Task Scope Honesty:** The detector is described solely as an **image-level binary classifier**. All claims of measured pixel-level localization IoU have been completely purged from the manuscript.
4. **Readiness Determination:** The paper represents a thoroughly audited, scientifically honest, and reproducible manuscript. It candidly reports completed descriptive and baseline results while identifying pending full-scale multi-seed ablations and external benchmarks.
