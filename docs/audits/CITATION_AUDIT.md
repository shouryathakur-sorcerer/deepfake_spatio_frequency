## 2. Claim-to-Source Alignment Verification

1. **Prior Art on WPD:** The paper strictly attributes the introduction of wavelet packets in deepfake forensics to Wolter et al. (2022) [Ref 6]. No claim of inventing WPD for deepfakes is made.
2. **Prior Art on Spatial-Frequency Fusion & Enhancement:** Attributed to Wang et al. (2023) [Ref 9], Wang et al. (2024) [Ref 10], and Gao et al. (2024) [Ref 11]. The paper positions its contribution as the controlled, matched ablation of this specific combination.
3. **Generalization Vulnerability:** Attributed to Kashiani et al. (2025) [Ref 5] and Chandra et al. (2026) [Ref 13], acknowledging that near-ceiling in-domain metrics on FFHQ vs StyleGAN2 drop substantially in realistic deployment.
4. **Compression Distinction:** Properly separates libjpeg DCT image quantization from Rössler et al.'s H.264 video compression [Ref 1].
